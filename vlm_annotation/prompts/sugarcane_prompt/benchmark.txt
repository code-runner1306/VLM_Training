You are an expert benchmark judge evaluating a synthetic VLM annotation of a SUGARCANE disease image.

GROUND-TRUTH DISEASE CLASS:
"{DISEASE_NAME}"

CANDIDATE VLM ANNOTATION:
{CANDIDATE_JSON}

The candidate annotation was generated from a sugarcane image.

Your primary task is to evaluate whether the annotation is VISUALLY GROUNDED and follows the Sugarcane Visual Annotation Specification.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT reward the candidate merely because its observations are typical of "{DISEASE_NAME}".

The ground-truth class is metadata.

A candidate receives credit only when its claims are supported by the actual image.

Evaluate the following dimensions:

1. VISUAL OBSERVATION ACCURACY — 30 points

Evaluate whether the candidate:

* Describes features actually visible in the image
* Correctly describes visible sugarcane plant organs
* Accurately describes visible colors
* Accurately describes visible shapes
* Accurately describes visible textures
* Accurately describes spatial distribution
* Avoids generic or fabricated descriptions

2. SUGARCANE MORPHOLOGY AWARENESS — 20 points

Evaluate whether the candidate correctly identifies visible sugarcane structures such as:

* Leaves
* Leaf blade
* Midrib
* Parallel venation
* Leaf margins
* Leaf sheath
* Stalk/stem
* Nodes
* Internodes
* Internal stalk tissue when actually visible

Do not penalize the candidate for omitting a structure that is not visible.

3. VISUAL EVIDENCE QUALITY — 20 points

Evaluate whether the annotation:

* Uses specific image-grounded descriptions
* Identifies where visible changes occur
* Describes color differences clearly
* Describes shape and spatial patterns when observable
* Provides useful visual evidence rather than generic statements

4. HALLUCINATION AND UNSUPPORTED CLAIMS — 20 points

Give a high score when the annotation contains no unsupported claims.

Penalize claims that:

* Describe symptoms that are not visible
* Assume disease-specific symptoms
* Infer invisible internal tissue
* Infer microscopic features
* Infer pathogens
* Infer causes
* Infer environmental conditions
* Infer treatment or management
* Infer disease progression
* Generalize a local observation to the entire plant
* Invent lesion sizes without reliable scale
* Claim a texture that cannot be visually determined
* Claim a feature solely because it is typical of the ground-truth disease

IMPORTANT:
Explicit uncertainty should NOT be penalized when the image genuinely does not provide enough information.

Unsupported certainty SHOULD be penalized.

5. SCHEMA AND METADATA VALIDITY — 10 points

Check whether:

* The output is valid JSON
* Required fields are present
* Field types are appropriate
* The "disease" field exactly matches "{DISEASE_NAME}"
* No unnecessary conversational text is present

DO NOT evaluate whether the candidate correctly diagnosed the disease.

The disease label is already supplied as ground truth.

Instead evaluate whether the visual evidence in the annotation is accurate and genuinely observable.

SCORING:

visual_observation_score: 0-30
sugarcane_morphology_score: 0-20
visual_evidence_score: 0-20
hallucination_score: 0-20
schema_score: 0-10

total_score = sum of the five scores.
